This is a TEST copy for MRZ and SA ID barcode reading. It works on the back of an Emirates ID and other GCC IDs, on the photo page of a passport, and on the thin barcode on the back of a South African smart ID. A PDF scan is also fine. It runs only on this device. A draft is kept separately from the live Patient details form. Check every field by hand.

Leave blank what you do not know — blank fields are left out of the message. If you change a form field after editing the message, the message is rebuilt from the form.
