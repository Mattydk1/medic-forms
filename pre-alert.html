Please read this first

This is only a quick-reference aid
Follow your company’s own medical and referral protocols, and always confirm with the receiving hospital. Do not use this page as the official destination list.

The UAE does not publish a public hospital catchment map like the Gauteng EMS tool. Hospitals here are ranked by straight-line distance (as the crow flies), not by road time, and not by any official catchment. In the desert, driving can take much longer than the straight-line figure suggests.

Pins for work bases are approximate public locations (a town, field, plant or camp area). They are not the exact clinic gate. For a life-threatening emergency in the UAE, call ambulance 998 or emergency 999.

With this turned on, Zayed Military Hospital is highlighted in the list, with the straight-line distance from your chosen point. For a critical patient, the nearest emergency department may still be the safer first stop — follow your protocol.

Short notes on each pin:

      
    
    
    

    
    
      
    Hospitals
    
      
    Only hospitals with a public emergency department are listed. Phone numbers are official switchboards or national emergency numbers, not unconfirmed direct ED lines.
    
      
    
      
    Sheikh Shakhbout Medical City is the published trauma centre for the Abu Dhabi region. Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi is the official chest pain and stroke centre for Abu Dhabi. Al Dhafra Hospitals (SEHA / PureHealth) run Madinat Zayed, Ghayathi, Mirfa, Al Sila, Liwa and Delma. Ruwais Hospital is in the ADNOC Ruwais area and is not on that current SEHA list. Delma Hospital is on Delma Island, so it is not used for the onshore nearest-hospital list.
    
    
    

    
    
      
    What to double-check
    
      
      
        
    • Coast Ghumais: on the planner, but no separate public location was found. Confirm whether it is a distinct site next to Ras Ghumais.

    • Al Hairah: this is still a probable name match in Sharjah (Al Heera). Confirm it is the compound you actually work at. Al Burair now uses the official Google Maps pin you provided, in southern Al Dhafra — not the Sharjah village of the same name, and not Al Burairat in Ras Al Khaimah.
      • 
        
    • Fujairah: the exact NGC clinic was not found. The pin is the port oil terminal, which may be wrong if your base is in the city.
      • 
        
    • Oil-field camps: Shah, ASAB, Sahil and Buhasa now use the official Google Maps pins you provided. Al Dabiya is still an approximate field or accommodation pin — the clinic gate may be a few kilometres away.
      • 
        
    • Phone numbers: use 998 or 999 in an emergency. Hospital numbers on this page are switchboards from official sites. Confirm the current ED number with your control room before you rely on it.
      • 
        
    • Military referrals: military patients usually go to Zayed Military Hospital, depending on how serious the illness or injury is. Critical cases may need the nearest emergency department first, following your protocol.
      • 
        
    • Driving time: always check a live route. Straight-line kilometres are only a first look.
